How the leaderboards work

Each board ranks players by one stat: money, playtime, kills, deaths, shards, blocks placed, blocks broken, mobs killed or the bounty on their head. Open a board to see the full ranking, then open any player to see the rest of their stats and how they have changed over time.

Rankings move constantly. Money in particular can swing by billions in a day from a single trade, while playtime and blocks only ever climb. When a player suddenly drops off a board or falls to the bottom, it usually means the server reset their stats or removed them, often after a ban.
